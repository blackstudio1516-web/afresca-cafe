About Afresca

More Than Just A Cafe.

Afresca is a cafe in Nashik made for easy mornings, long catch-ups and everything in between. Think good coffee, cool drinks, comfort food and sweet endings, served in a bright, relaxed space.

Come for a quick coffee, stay for the pizza, or bring friends for dessert. This is a place to slow down and enjoy fresh flavours.
